# Citations

If you use HMM Homologue Finder in your research, please cite **this tool**
(see [`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff)) **and** the underlying methods and databases.
Each run's `METHODS.md` lists only the tools/databases that run actually used.

## Software
- **HMMER 3.4** (hmmbuild / hmmsearch / hmmscan) — Eddy SR (2011) Accelerated profile HMM searches. *PLoS Comput Biol* 7:e1002195.
- **MAFFT v7** — Katoh K & Standley DM (2013) MAFFT multiple sequence alignment software version 7. *Mol Biol Evol* 30:772–780.
- **trimAl** — Capella-Gutiérrez S, Silla-Martínez JM, Gabaldón T (2009) *Bioinformatics* 25:1972–1973.
- **Prodigal** — Hyatt D et al. (2010) *BMC Bioinformatics* 11:119.
- **SeqKit** — Shen W et al. (2016) *PLoS One* 11:e0163962.
- **CD-HIT** — Fu L et al. (2012) *Bioinformatics* 28:3150–3152.
- **IQ-TREE 2** — Minh BQ et al. (2020) *Mol Biol Evol* 37:1530–1534.
  - **ModelFinder** — Kalyaanamoorthy S et al. (2017) *Nat Methods* 14:587–589.
  - **UFBoot2** (ultrafast bootstrap) — Hoang DT et al. (2018) *Mol Biol Evol* 35:518–522.
- **MEME Suite** — Bailey TL et al. (2009) *Nucleic Acids Res* 37:W202–W208.
- **FIMO** — Grant CE, Bailey TL, Noble WS (2011) *Bioinformatics* 27:1017–1018.
- **clinker** — Gilchrist CLM & Chooi YH (2021) *Bioinformatics* 37:2473–2475.
- **MMseqs2** (optional) — Steinegger M & Söding J (2017) *Nat Biotechnol* 35:1026–1028.
- **Biopython** — Cock PJA et al. (2009) *Bioinformatics* 25:1422–1423.
